# Content License (proposed — pending owner ratification)

**Status: proposal by the engineering agent. The repository owner has not yet ratified a content license. Nothing in `modules/`, `experiences/`, or `registries/` may be treated as licensed content until the owner decides, because there is no approved scientific content yet either.**

## Proposed terms

- Original **human-authored** learning content (lessons, concept definitions, exercises, editorial text): **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original **code**: Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`).
- **Third-party material** keeps its own license and attribution; see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Nothing with unknown rights is imported.

## What this does NOT cover

- **There is no approved scientific content in this repository yet.** All records are drafts or engineering fixtures. An empty or draft record grants no rights to anything.
- **Third-party scientific text, images, and data are not covered by CC BY 4.0.** Publicly readable is not freely redistributable. Each record carries its own rights decision (`link-only`, `metadata-only`, `permitted-redistribution`, `restricted`, `prohibited`); unknown rights block import.
- **OpenStax Astronomy 2e** is link-only by default (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 plus AI-ingestion restrictions). Its text must not be ingested into this repository or any model workflow without prior written permission.
- **Unified Astronomy Thesaurus (UAT)**, if redistributed or adapted, must keep its CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution and share-alike terms, tracked independently per pinned version.
- **NASA/ESA media**: educational-use permissions vary per asset and are not blanket rights; third-party material inside agency pages has its own restrictions. Every asset must be verified before use.

## Decision required from the owner

Choose and ratify: (a) keep CC BY 4.0 for original content, (b) choose another license, (c) keep content All-Rights-Reserved until review capacity exists. Until then this file documents intent only.
