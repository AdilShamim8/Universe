# ADR 0006 — Neutral internal name; no public branding yet

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-17)

## Context
"Open Cosmos" is an existing space company. Public branding, domain registration, and repository renaming require owner decisions not yet made.

## Decision
Internal codename `universe-platform` everywhere; no logo, no domain, no renaming. The footer disclaims affiliation and makes no accuracy/completeness claims. Public release is gated on a cleared name, ratified licenses, hosting approval, and registered reviewers.

## Consequences
Zero trademark collision risk while decisions are pending; the site is honest about its preview status on every page.
