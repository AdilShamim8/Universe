# 0.2.0 — The curated product (2026-11-19)

The site stops being an empty scaffold and becomes a real learning platform. The
engineered backbone (contracts, registry, fail-closed projection) is unchanged;
what changed is that the site now has content worth reading, governed by the new
two-layer content policy ([docs/content-workflow.md](../content-workflow.md)).

## What ships

- **Timeline** — 77 events across 12 eras, from the Planck epoch to conditional
  futures. Every event carries its source URL, date precision, and stated
  uncertainty; ΛCDM/Planck 2018 anchors the model-dependent ages. Era navigation
  with original SVG glyphs per era.
- **Academy** — 11 learning pathways (157 verified resources) from L0 curious to
  L5 research: what to study, where, in what order, and what comes next.
  Original subject emblems; prerequisite and next-subject links.
- **Research** — 8 fields with 46 arXiv-verified papers (titles/authors/dates
  scraped from abstract pages, not typed from memory), 17 datasets, 19 tools,
  official priorities and published open questions with citations. Sortable
  paper lists.
- **Library** — 79 resources in 10 categories with difficulty filter, license
  notes, and source lines.
- **Explore** — planet facts parsed live from the NASA NSSDC fact sheet; 10
  planet detail pages with fact tables and size bars; exoplanet comparison
  table with masses/radii/periods queried from the Exoplanet Archive TAP
  service; 15 missions on a decades rail; log-scale scale-of-the-universe
  visualizer; PlanetBars diameter chart.
- **Design** — cinematic cosmic identity: Space Grotesk / Inter / IBM Plex Mono
  (self-hosted), starfield backdrop, focus-polished sticky header, print
  stylesheet that inlines source URLs on paper.
- **Imagery** — Hubble near-infrared Pillars of Creation homepage hero (NASA/
  ESA/Hubble Heritage Team) with LQIP blur-up and credit line; original SVG art
  everywhere else.
- **SEO/distribution** — sitemap, canonical URLs, Open Graph + JSON-LD, RSS
  feed, favicon, humans.txt.
- **Tooling** — `scripts/verify-curated-urls.mjs` re-verifies every curated
  link (retry, transient-vs-dead classification); 27 curated contract tests;
  23 browser e2e tests; build-time provenance enforcement.

## What did not change

The no-LLM-generated-science policy and the human-review requirement for
original scientific explanations. Zero approved original records exist; the
footer says so plainly. Curated links and imported facts are attribution, not
assertion — that is the whole basis of this release.

## Numbers

37 public pages · 301 external URLs verified (297 hard-verified, 4 transient) ·
146 unit/integration tests · 23 e2e tests · 0 fabricated sources.
